#description
The result of our work is a **modern, bold and energetic website** that captures the character of the Grow brand and the personality of its team far better than the previous version of the site. The new UX/UI design brings clarity to the communication, makes it easier to navigate the company's broad range of services, and gives the brand a more contemporary look. The multicolored identity highlights the diversity of competencies, the wide range of services and the many directions of the company's work, while bold color combinations and unobvious visual solutions present Grow as a creative, open and unconventional brand - just as it is in reality.
<br>
<br>
The design was prepared with **implementation within the WordPress builder used by the client** in mind, which required us to adapt the **UX/UI design** to its capabilities and limitations. Instead of our standard approach based on a **custom WordPress implementation**, we precisely translated the design into the existing environment, preserving its character, responsiveness and visual consistency. As a result, the client received a modern and polished website without having to change the technology they wanted to keep using.
